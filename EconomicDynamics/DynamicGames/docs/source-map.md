# Source map: Miao Chapter 21

Jianjun Miao, *Economic Dynamics in Discrete Time* (draft of 21 April 2013), Chapter 21, Dynamic
Games, pp. 737–784. Lean names are relative to `Miao.DynamicGames`. Page references are the draft's
running heads. The general repeated-game theory is built on `rg_Strat`/`rg_B`, public monitoring on
`DGPublicGame`, stochastic games on `DGStochGame`, and the credible-policy economy on
`CGProfile`/`cg_B`. Departures from the book are in [corrections](corrections.md).
