# Source map: Miao Chapter 12

Jianjun Miao, *Economic Dynamics in Discrete Time* (draft of 21 April 2013), Chapter 12, Structural
Estimation, pp. 385–410. Lean names are relative to `Miao.StructuralEstimation`. The data are a
stationary ergodic process on the path space of Chapter 4 (`IsStationary`, `IsErgodicProcess`);
convergence in probability and in distribution are Mathlib's `TendstoInMeasure` and
`TendstoInDistribution`. Departures from the book are in [corrections](corrections.md).
