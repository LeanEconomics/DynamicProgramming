# Corrections and conventions

This file lists the places where a Lean statement differs from the book: a claim corrected, a
hypothesis made explicit, a proof replaced, or a convention chosen. Six claims are false as
printed. Each is refuted in Lean by a counterexample of this project, and a corrected statement is
proved: Theorem 19.1.2 with `n(x) = 0` allowed, the bound (19.25), the irreducibility claim for the
walk on `ℝ²₊`, the coercivity claim for the bilinear model, (V4) with linear test functions in
STEP 2 of Appendix B.2, and the busy-period decomposition (19.84). Further entries concern
misprints, gaps in proofs and remarks: (19.50), the drift display of p. 498, the justification of
(19.77), the irreducibility of the invasion model, its left continuity (19.29) and test set, the
petite sets of p. 506 and the constant of (19.81). Every entry was checked against the page images
of the book, not only its text layer. The book's statements are paraphrased. The last section
lists what is not formalised.

## Conventions

* Transition laws are Mathlib's kernels and `Pⁿ` is the kernel power. Test functions and drift
  constants take values in `[0, ∞]` (`ℝ≥0∞`) except where the book's argument is real: the mixed
  drift of §19.3.1 (`driftR`, real `V ≥ 0`), Theorems 19.2.2 and 19.2.5 (real `Vₖ`), and the
  SETAR test functions. The chain lives on the path space `ℕ → X` with laws `P_x = pathKernel P x`
  and the natural filtration `natFilt` (carried).
* "a.s. [`P_*`]" is "under `P_x` for every `x`". Conditional expectations appear only in
  integrated form over the sets of `𝓕ₖ^Φ` (above).
* `‖μ − ν‖` in (19.16) and Theorem 19.2.6 is the total variation norm
  `fNorm (fun _ => 1) μ ν = sup_{|g| ≤ 1} |μ(g) − ν(g)|` (carried), which is at most `2`;
  `‖·‖_f` in Theorem 19.2.3 is `fNorm f`.
* The constant `d` of (19.46) is written `B`, since `d` is used for the truncation level in
  §19.3.2.
* SETAR regimes are indexed by `Fin (m + 1)`: the book's regime `1` is `0` and regime `M` is
  `Fin.last m`.
* The following misprints are read silently. In (19.65) the sum runs over `j ≥ i + 1`, not
  `j ≥ k + 1`. In (19.67) `β(w)` is `Σⱼ jΛⱼ(w, X)`, not `Λⱼ(x, X)`. In Theorem 19.2.5 the
  conclusion is for `P_x`, not `P_{x₀}` (the statement is for all `x` with `V₀(x)`). On p. 507,
  `Pᵗ(x, y)` is `P(R_t ≤ y | R₀ = x)`. STEP 1 of Appendix B.2 cites Theorem 6.3.6, which is
  Proposition 6.3.6 (`proposition_6_3_6`, carried).
* The PDF text layer drops several symbols of this chapter: the integral signs of (19.1), (19.6),
  (19.10), (19.12) and (19.15), the prime of `V′` in (19.17), the `≠` in the independence of the
  SETAR noises, and the superscripts of `Φ^{[0]}` in (19.81). Every statement recorded here was
  checked against the page images. The `Pⁿ` of (19.50) and the `3ε³Aₖ²` of p. 498 are as printed.
* The carried modules are copied from the earlier projects without change apart from the
  namespace, the imports and a "Copied for Chapter 19" note (see the [README](../README.md)). The
  corrections they contain belong to Chapters 1–18, are recorded in their module docstrings and in
  those projects, and are not repeated here.
