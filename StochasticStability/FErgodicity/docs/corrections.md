# Corrections and conventions

This file lists the places where a Lean statement differs from the book: a claim corrected, a
hypothesis made explicit, a proof replaced, or a convention chosen. Five of the claims below are
refuted in Lean by a counterexample of this project, and for each of them a corrected statement is
proved. Two further entries correct arguments of §14.5 whose conclusions are true; there is no
counterexample to give. For the other entries the table says why no counterexample is given. The
last section lists what is not formalised.

## Conventions

* Transition laws are Mathlib's kernels, and `Pⁿ` is the kernel power. `μP` is `P ∘ₘ μ`. Measures,
  kernels and the functions `f`, `V`, `s` take values in `[0, ∞]` (`ℝ≥0∞`).
* `fNorm f μ ν` is `sup_{|g| ≤ f} |μ(g) − ν(g)|` over measurable `g : X → ℝ` with `|g| ≤ f` that
  are integrable for both measures (`IsFTest`). It is valued in `[0, ∞]`, so it is defined without
  `μ(f) < ∞`; it is finite when `μ(f)` and `ν(f)` are (`fNorm_le_add`). The book's `‖ν‖_f` for a
  signed measure `ν` is `fNorm f μ₁ μ₂` for `ν = μ₁ − μ₂`. Sums of `f`-norms, as in (14.5), (14.13)
  and (14.23)–(14.25), are taken in `[0, ∞]`.
* `fSumReturn P B f x = E_x[Σ_{k<τ_B} f(Φ_k)]` with `τ_B` the first return time `firstReturn B`
  (carried), as in (14.2), (14.10) and (14.19). `G_B(x, f)` is `gKernelFn P B f x` and `U_B(x, f)`
  is `tabooPotFn P B f x` (carried). `IsFRegularSet P ψ f C` includes the measurability of `C`.
  `IsFRegularOn P ψ f S` (a countable union of `f`-regular sets equal to `S`) is the book's "`Φ`
  restricted to `S` is `f`-regular".
* The atom of §14.1 is a measurable set `α` with a point `a ∈ α`. `tabooKernel P hα n a` (carried)
  is `_αPⁿ⁺¹(a, ·)` and `tabooSeq P hα λ n` (carried) is `λ _αPⁿ⁺¹`, so (14.6) and (14.14) are
  stated at time `n + 1`. Sums over `n ≥ 1`, as in (14.13) and (14.24), are written `Σ_{n ≥ 0}` of
  the terms at `n + 1`. The sums of (14.5), (14.25) and Theorem 14.1.4 run over `n ≥ 0`.
* The cycle of a chain of period `d` is `D : ZMod d → Set X` (`IsCycle`, carried), and
  `cycleInv π D i` (carried) is `πᵢ = d π(· ∩ Dᵢ)`. From `x ∈ Dᵢ` the chain is in `D_{i+r}` at time
  `nd + r`, so the limit in (14.26) from `Dᵢ` at phase `r` is `π_{i+r}` (`theorem_14_3_6_i`). In the
  book's labelling, where `x ∈ D_r` and the phase is also `r`, the limit `π_r` is the cycle
  probability `d π(· ∩ D_{2r})` of the class `2r` (`theorem_14_3_6_i_book`). The classes are
  indexed by `ℤ/d` rather than `1 ≤ i ≤ d`, and in (14.27) the average runs over `0 ≤ r < d`, where
  the book writes `1 ≤ r ≤ d`.
* (V3) for the skeleton is stated with `½ f^{(m)}` (`driftV3_skeleton`), as in the book's proof of
  Theorem 14.2.9, and `IsFRegularSet.of_half` returns to `f^{(m)}`.
* In §14.5, `renMeas Γ μ` is the renewal measure `μ ∗ U = Σ_{m ≥ 0} μ ∗ Γ^{m∗}`, the expected number
  of epochs `Z_{m+1} = x + Y₀ + ⋯ + Y_{m−1}` (`resEpoch`, carried) in a set, for a first epoch
  `x ~ μ` and increments `Γ^{⊗ℕ}` (`incr Γ`). The book's `Γ₀ ∗ U` is `renMeas Γ Γ₀`. `convAt ρ g t`
  is `∫ g(t − u) ρ(du)`, a real number. `convSupDiff f ρ₁ ρ₂ t` is the supremum in (14.45), valued
  in `[0, ∞]`. The suprema in (14.36) and (14.40) are taken in `[0, ∞]` through `ENNReal.ofReal`
  (Mathlib). `β` is `renMean Γ = ∫ y Γ(dy)`, and `β⁻¹` is `((renMean Γ).toReal)⁻¹`. `Γₑ` is
  `eqLaw Γ`, the normalised invariant measure `fwdSkelInv Γ` (carried) of (10.36). Lebesgue measure is
  `volume` (Mathlib), and `Γ₀ ∗ U(t + B)` is `(renMeas Γ Γ₀).real {u | u − t ∈ B}`.
* The forward recurrence time `δ`-skeleton is `fwdSkelKernel Γ δ` (carried), and its restart from
  `(−∞, 0]` is `fwdChain Γ δ`. Spread-out laws are `IsSpreadOut` (carried, (RW2)).
* The carried modules are copied from `Ergodicity` without change apart from the namespace and the
  imports (see the [README](../README.md)). The corrections they contain belong to Chapters 1–13,
  are recorded in their module docstrings and in those projects, and are not repeated here, except
  where a Chapter 14 entry above uses them.
