# Source map

Meyn and Tweedie, *Markov Chains and Stochastic Stability*, 2nd edition (Cambridge University
Press, 2009): Chapter 5, pp. 96–122. Equation numbers and assumption labels are the book's. Lean
names are relative to `MeynTweedie.PseudoAtoms`. The split-chain results take a minorization
`M : Minorization P` and are in the namespace `Minorization`. Names marked "carried" are in the
modules copied from the Chapter 1–4 projects (see the [README](../README.md)). Where the Lean
statement departs from the book, see [corrections](corrections.md).
