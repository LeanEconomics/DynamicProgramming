# Corrections and conventions

This file lists the places where a Lean statement differs from the book: a claim corrected, a
hypothesis made explicit, a proof replaced, or a convention chosen. Eight of the claims below are
false as printed. Seven are refuted in Lean by a counterexample of this project and one by an exact
computation, and for each a corrected statement is proved. One further entry corrects the book's
proof of Theorem 15.4.2, whose statement is true; there is no counterexample to give. For the other
entries the table says why no counterexample is given. The book's statements are paraphrased. The
last section lists what is not formalised.

## Conventions

* Transition laws are Mathlib's kernels, and `Pⁿ` is the kernel power. `μP` is `P ∘ₘ μ`. Measures,
  kernels, the functions `f`, `V` and the rates `r`, `κ`, `ρ` take values in `[0, ∞]` (`ℝ≥0∞`). A
  rate is a number `1 < r` with `r ≠ ∞`. `tpow r t = rᵗ` for `t ∈ ℕ ∪ {∞}`, with `r^∞ = ∞`, so
  `kendallMoment P A r x = E_x[r^{τ_A}]` is infinite as soon as `τ_A = ∞` with positive probability.
* The three geometric sums are `geomSum P B r f x = E_x[Σ_{k=0}^{τ_B − 1} f(Φ_k) rᵏ]`,
  `geomTaboo P B r f x = U_B^{(r)}(x, f) = E_x[Σ_{k=1}^{τ_B} f(Φ_k) rᵏ]` and
  `geomG P B r f x = G_B^{(r)}(x, f) = E_x[Σ_{k=0}^{σ_B} f(Φ_k) rᵏ]`, with `τ_B` the first return
  time `firstReturn B` and `σ_B` the first hitting time `firstHit B` (carried). `f`-Kendall sets
  (15.22) and `f`-geometric regularity (15.36) are stated with `geomSum`, as in the book. At `r = 1`
  the sums are `fSumReturn`, `tabooPotFn` and `gKernelFn` of Chapters 11 and 14 (carried). The
  kernel `U_C^{(r)}(x, B)` of (15.23) is `geomTaboo P C r (B.indicator 1) x`.
* (V4) is `DriftV4 P V ρ b C`, `PV ≤ ρV + b 1_C` with `ρ = 1 − β`, which avoids subtraction. A
  constant `β ≥ 1` in the book's form implies (V4) with `ρ = 0`, since `PV ≥ 0`.
* `IsKendall P A κ`, `IsFKendall P f A κ` and `IsFGeomRegularSet P ψ f A` include the measurability
  of `A`, and their rates are finite. `IsFGeomRegularSet` quantifies over measurable `B` with
  `ψ(B) > 0`. `IsFGeomRegularOn P f S` (a petite `C` and `κ` with the sums finite on `S` and bounded
  on `C`) is the book's `f`-geometric regularity of `Φ` restricted to `S`, and
  `IsFGeomRegularChain P f` is the case `S = X`.
* `τ_C(n)` is `returnTimeK C n` (carried), with `τ_C(0) = 0` and `τ_C(1) = τ_C`, as on p. 374.
* The atom of §15.1 is a measurable set `α` with a point `a ∈ α`. `tabooKernel P hα n a` (carried)
  is `_αPⁿ⁺¹(a, ·)`, so sums over `n ≥ 1`, as in (15.9), (15.18), (15.19) and Theorem 15.4.1, are
  written `Σ_{n ≥ 0}` of the terms at `n + 1`. `IsGeomErgodicAtom P π α` asks for the summability at
  every `a ∈ α`; on an atom the sequence does not depend on `a` (`IsAtom.atomRenewal_eq`).
* The split chain of `M : Minorization P` (carried; a set `C`, `δ ∈ (0, 1)` and a probability `ν`
  with `P(x, ·) ≥ δν` on `C`) lives on `X × Bool`, with the atom `α̌ = C × {true}` (`M.checkAlpha`)
  and the split initial law `x*` (`M.splitK x`). `Minorization.IsConcentrated` (carried) is the
  book's `ν(Cᶜ) = 0`. `Minorization.restrict` restricts a minorization to a measurable subset of
  `C`.
* The forward recurrence time chain has renewal state `0` where the book writes `1`. The Bernoulli
  walk on `ℤ₊` is `reflectedWalkKernel (bernoulliStep p)` (carried) on `ℕ`, where `x − 1` is
  truncated at `0`.
* The carried modules are copied from `FErgodicity` without change apart from the namespace and the
  imports, and the four module docstrings named in the [README](../README.md). The corrections they
  contain belong to Chapters 1–14, are recorded in their module docstrings and in those projects,
  and are not repeated here, except where a Chapter 15 entry above uses them.
