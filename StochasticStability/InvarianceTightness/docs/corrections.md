# Corrections and conventions

This file lists the places where a Lean statement differs from the book: a claim corrected, a
hypothesis made explicit, a proof replaced, or a convention chosen. Three of the claims below are
refuted in Lean by a counterexample, and for each of them a corrected statement is proved. A
fourth, the remark (12.22), is refuted by a counterexample carried from Chapter 6. For the other
entries the table says why no counterexample is given. The last section lists what is not
formalised.

## Conventions

* Transition laws are Mathlib's kernels, and `Pⁿ` is the kernel power. `μP` is `P ∘ₘ μ`. Mathlib's
  composition `η ∘ₖ κ` is "first `κ`, then `η`", so the book's `ΠP` is `P ∘ₖ Π` and its `PΠ` is
  `Π ∘ₖ P`. Measures and kernels take values in `[0, ∞]` (`ℝ≥0∞`), and "`μ(A) < ∞`" is `μ A ≠ ∞`
  or `μ A < ∞`.
* `P̄ⁿ` is `cesaroKernel P n`, the sampled kernel of the uniform distribution on `{1, …, n}`
  (`sampledKernel`, `uniformDist`, carried); `P̄ⁿ f` for a real `f` is `cesaroFun P n f`. `K_{a_ε}`
  is `resolvent P ε` (carried), with `ε : ℝ≥0∞`. `C_c(X)` is Mathlib's `C_c(X, ℝ)` and bounded
  continuous functions are `X →ᵇ ℝ`; vague convergence is convergence of `∫ f` for every
  `f ∈ C_c(X)`, and a vague limit point is `vagueLimit μs hμ U`.
* "Feller" is `IsWeakFeller P` (carried), the lower semicontinuity of `x ↦ P(x, O)` for open `O`;
  an e-chain is `IsEChain P` (carried); boundedness in probability is `IsBoundedInProbability P`
  (carried) and boundedness in probability on average is `IsBoundedInProbabilityOnAverage P`, both
  with tightness in the sense (12.1), `1 − ε ≤ liminf_k`; coercive functions are `IsCoercive V`
  (carried), `V : X → ℝ≥0` with precompact sublevel sets; tightness of a family of measures is
  Mathlib's `IsTightMeasureSet`.
* The functions `h` of §12.2 take values in `[0, ∞]` with `h ≤ 1`. `P I_w u` is
  `killOp P w u`, `Σₖ (P I_w)ᵏ φ` is `geomOp P w φ`, `P I_{1−h}` is `killKernel P h`, `U_h` is

* The limit kernel `Π` of an e-chain `he : IsEChain P` is `eChainLimit he`, and `u = Π(·, X)` is
  `eChainMass he`. Limit measures along a subsequence `σ` on which the averages converge
  (`CesaroConverges P σ`) are `limMeasure P σ x`.
* The linear state space model is `lssMatrixKernel Γ F G` and the bilinear model
  `bilinearKernel Γ θ b` (carried). The adaptive control model has state `(Σ, θ̃, Y)` in `ℝ³`;
  `sacKernel Γ α σz σw` takes the law `Γ` of `(Z, W)` on `ℝ²` and `sacGaussianKernel α σz σw`
