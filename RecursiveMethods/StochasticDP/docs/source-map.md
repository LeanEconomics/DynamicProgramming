# Source map

Stokey and Lucas with Prescott, *Recursive Methods in Economic Dynamics* (Harvard University Press,
1989), Chapter 9, pp. 239–287. Lean names are relative to `StokeyLucasPrescott.StochasticDP`.
Where the Lean statement departs from the book, see [corrections](corrections.md).

The results are proved for the book's second formulation (action space `Y`, law of motion
`x′ = φ(x, y, z′)`, pp. 255–259). The first formulation is `Model.first` (`Y = X`, `φ = y`), and
its theorems are corollaries.
