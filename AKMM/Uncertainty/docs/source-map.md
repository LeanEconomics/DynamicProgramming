# Source map: AKMM Chapter 7

Marina Azzimonti, Per Krusell, Alisdair McKay and Toshihiko Mukoyama, *Macroeconomics* (draft of
December 2025), Chapter 7, Uncertainty, pp. 187–215, and Appendix 7.A, pp. 840–841. Page
references are the draft's running heads. Lean names are relative to `AKMM.Uncertainty`.
Departures from the book are in [corrections](corrections.md).

`BPTree`, `bp_nodeSum`, `bp_condExp` and their lemmas (tower property, iterated expectations,
`bp_markovTree`, `eq_13_7_markov`). Miao `MarkovProcesses` (`Miao.MarkovProcesses`):
`mc_IsStationary`, `mc_IsIrreducible`, `mc_pow_add_ge`, `mc_exists_stationary`,
`mc_irreducible_stationary`, `theorem_3_1_3`. Miao `RationalExpectations`

`var_stationary_moments`, `var_lyapMap`, `var_lyapSol`, `lyap_fixed`, `var_mean_rec`,
`var_impulse_response`, `IsStableMatrix`, `isStableMatrix_of_tendsto`, `isStableMatrix_iff_roots`,
`tendsto_of_isStableMatrix`. Obstfeld–Rogoff `InternationalFinancialMarkets`: `Spanning.spans_iff_rank`.
AKMM Chapter 4 (`AKMM.DynamicOptimization`): `crra`, `example42_ratio`, `example42_eis`,
`eq_4_29`, `slopeRoot`, `slopeRootOther`, `slopeRoot_isRoot`, `slopeRoot_mem`. AKMM Chapter 5
(`AKMM.CompetitiveEquilibrium`): `ce_foc_of_perturb`, `ce_tsum_perturb`, `ce_log_tangent`, and
`euler_crs` (cited for Euler's theorem). Miao `MarkovProcesses` also supplies, for the

`ConditionMFor`, `example_3_3_1` (an accessible atom gives Condition M), `theorem_3_3_1_invariant`

`Applications` (its income-fluctuation results, Lemma 8.3.3 and Proposition 8.3.1, are for i.i.d.
