The post is cited by paragraph: "#3 ¶4" is the fourth paragraph of post #3, counting the
displayed transition matrix with the text around it; "PS", "PPS" and "PPPS" are the
postscripts. Each claim is quoted by a few words at most and otherwise paraphrased. The
"Reading" is the precise statement that the Lean results prove. Lean names are relative to
`RQMCMarkov.MarkovDriving`; names from Mathlib are marked as such.

Notation. States are `Fin n`, `0`-indexed: post #3's `x_j` is `j − 1`. Time starts at `0`, and
`u t` drives the transition `t → t + 1`, so post #3's `x^t` and `u^t` are `x (t − 1)` and
`u (t − 1)`. The inverse-CDF update is `step P i u` and `cs_{ij}` is `cumProb P i j`. A path
driven by one sequence is `drivenPath P x₀ u`; a path driven by per-state stacks `v i k` is
`stackPath P x₀ v`; the rotor walk is `rotorWalk L c θ x₀`. Over the horizon `T`,
`N_i(T) = occCount x i T` counts the times `t < T` with `x t = i`, and
`N_ij(T) = transCount x i j T` the transitions `t → t + 1`, `t < T`, from `i` to `j`. The
departure discrepancy is `D_i(g, T) = departureDiscrepancy P g x i T`. `π` is a stationary
distribution (`IsStationaryDistribution`, or `IsStationaryRow` in the rounding modules), `h`
solves Poisson's equation `h − Ph = 1_{s} − π_s`, and `M` bounds `|h|`. Every place where a Lean
statement departs from the post is listed in [corrections](corrections.md).
