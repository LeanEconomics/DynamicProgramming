# Source map

Robert Kirkby, "Simulating a Markov chain: MC, QMC, RQMC", VFI Toolkit discourse, topic 687,
posts #1 and #2 (28 October 2026),
<https://discourse.vfitoolkit.com/t/simulating-a-markov-chain-mc-qmc-rqmc/687>. The posts cite
L'Ecuyer, *Randomized Quasi-Monte Carlo: An Introduction for Practitioners* (MCQMC 2016 tutorial;
"L'Ecuyer 2016"), L'Ecuyer, Munger and Tuffin, "On the distribution of integration error by
randomly-shifted lattice rules", *Electronic Journal of Statistics* (2010), doi
10.1214/10-EJS574, and Loh, "On the asymptotic distribution of scrambled net quadrature",
*Annals of Statistics* (2003), doi 10.1214/aos/1059655914. Owen (1997) is the source of the
variance theory of scrambled nets. The module docstrings also cite Koksma (1942), van der Corput
(1935), Cranley and Patterson (1976), Niederreiter (1992) and Dick and Pillichshammer (2010).

The posts are cited by paragraph: "#1 ¶5" is the fifth paragraph of post #1, counting the
displayed formulas with the text around them; "PS" and "PPS" are the postscripts. Each claim is
quoted by a few words at most and otherwise paraphrased. The "Reading" is the precise statement
that the Lean results prove. Lean names are relative to `RQMCMarkov.PointSets`; names from
Mathlib are marked as such.

Notation: `U[0,1)` is `uniformIco = volume.restrict [0,1)` and `U[0,1)^s` is `uniformCube s`.
The estimator over a point set `x : Fin n → E` is `sampleMean n f x = n⁻¹ Σᵢ f(xᵢ)`, and over the
first `n` terms of a sequence `x : ℕ → ℝ` it is `qmcEstimate f x n`. `D*ₙ` is
`starDiscrepancy x n`, `V(f)` is `totalVariation f`, `φ` is `vanDerCorput`, the random shift
modulo 1 is `shiftMod x u` (one dimension) and `shiftMod1 y u` (`s` dimensions), the stratum map
is `stratumMap n i u = (i + u)/n`, and the base-2 digital shift is `digitalShift m y u` (on a
sequence, `digitalShiftSeq m x u`). Every place where a Lean statement departs from the posts is
listed in [corrections](corrections.md).
