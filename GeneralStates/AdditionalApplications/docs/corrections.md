# Corrections to the source

Places where a claim in Chapter 8 of Sargent and Stachurski, *Dynamic Programming*, Volume 2,
is imprecise as printed, relies on an unstated hypothesis, or where the Lean statement departs
from the printed one. In each case the Lean statement proves the corrected or stated version and
cites the original. Entries are recorded when found and revised if formalisation shows the finding
itself to be wrong.

## Errors and gaps

| Where | Book says | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ex 8.1.4, p. 248 | `(V, 𝕋)` is an ADP for `V = bcW`, `ibcW` and `ibcW₊`. | A policy operator with a discontinuous `σ` does not map `bcW` into itself: with `W = [0, 1]`, `β = 1/2`, `c = 0` and `σ = 𝟙{w ≥ 1/2}`, `T_σ 0 = 2w𝟙{w ≥ 1/2}` (`BoundedSearch.exercise_8_1_4_counterexample`). The Bellman operator does map the three spaces into themselves (`BoundedSearch.exercise_8_1_4_bellman`), and `(bW, 𝕋)` is an ADP (`BoundedSearch.exercise_8_1_4`). |
| Ex 8.1.24, p. 260 | `h_a ≤ h_b` pointwise on `ℝ`. | `h_a, h_b ∈ L¹(φ)` are equivalence classes; the order is `φ`-almost everywhere (`PersistentSearch.exercise_8_1_24`). |
| Ex 8.3.5, solution, p. 417 | `u(c) ≥ u(c₀) + u'(c₀)(c − c₀)`. | For concave `u` the tangent inequality is reversed. The argument goes through with `u(c) − u(c₀) ≥ u'(c)(c − c₀)` and `u'` decreasing: if `u' ≥ ε` everywhere then `u` is unbounded (`Growth.exercise_8_3_5`). |
| Ex 8.3.6, p. 285, and solution, p. 417 | `(y, c) ↦ B(y, c, v)` is continuous on `G` for all `v ∈ bX`; the solution uses "continuity of `v ∘ g`". | False for discontinuous `v`: with `v = 𝟙_(0, ∞)`, `B(y, c, v) = u(c) + β𝟙{c < y}` jumps at `c = y` (`Growth.exercise_8_3_6_false`). It holds for `v ∈ bcX`, by dominated convergence (`Growth.exercise_8_3_6`). |
| Prop 8.3.7, p. 285, and Ex 8.3.7 solution, p. 417 | The fundamental optimality properties hold and VFI, OPI and HPI converge (Proposition 7.2.2 with Assumption 7.2.3). | Assumption 7.2.3 is Exercise 8.3.6 for all `v ∈ bX`, which fails. Worse, the ADP on `bX` is not regular: `v = 𝟙_(0, ∞)` has no greedy policy, since `sup_{c < y} u(c) + β = u(y) + β` is not attained (`Growth.not_regular`). The fundamental optimality properties, `v* ∈ bcX` and geometric convergence of VFI on `bcX` hold (`Growth.proposition_8_3_7`); the OPI and HPI claims are not established. |
| Prop 8.3.9 (i), p. 286 | For increasing concave `v ∈ bcℝ₊`, the unique `v`-greedy `σ` is interior. | False: `v = 0` is increasing, concave and continuous, and its greedy policy is `σ(y) = y` (`Growth.proposition_8_3_9_not_interior`). What holds is `σ(y) > 0` for `y > 0`, from `u'(0) = ∞` (`Growth.argmax_pos`). Part (ii) holds: `Tv` is concave, strictly increasing and continuously differentiable on `(0, ∞)` with `(Tv)' = u' ∘ σ` (`Growth.proposition_8_3_9`); the proof uses only `σ(y) > 0`. Strict monotonicity of `σ` and strict concavity of `Tv`, for which the book refers to Stachurski (2022), are not formalised. |
| (8.53), p. 279; "Connection to the production chain", p. 281 | An `η ∈ (0, x̂)` with `c'(η) = δc'(0)` exists "whenever `x̂` is large enough"; with `x̂ = 1`, Lemma 8.3.5 gives Proposition 8.3.1. | With `x̂ = 1` such an `η` need not exist (when `c'(1) < δc'(0)`), so the alternative proof of Proposition 8.3.1 covers only part of the production chains. The formalisation assumes `η > 0` with `c'(x) ≤ δc'(0) ⟺ x ≤ η` on `[0, x̂]` (`NegDiscount.η_spec`), which (8.53) implies (`NegDiscount.η_spec_of_eq`) and which some `η` satisfies for every chain (`ProductionChain.exists_η`: the solution of `c'(η) = δc'(0)` when `δc'(0) ≤ c'(1)`, and `η = 1` otherwise, when every firm completes the good alone). Propositions 8.3.1–8.3.2 then hold for every chain. |
| Kσ, p. 288 | `Kσ(y)` is well defined: the integral term is continuous and strictly increasing in `c` and diverges as `c ↑ y`. | The integral `∫ (u' ∘ σ)(f(y − c)z) f'(y − c) z φ(dz)` can be infinite, since `u'` is unbounded near `0` and the shock can be small (for example `u'(c) ≍ c^{−0.9}`, `σ(x) ≍ x³` near `0`, and a density positive near `0`). Uniqueness of the solution (`Growth.solvesEuler_unique`) and the monotonicity of Exercise 8.3.10 hold wherever solutions exist; existence is not formalised. |
| §8.3.4, pp. 290–293 | `M` is a bijection from `V𝒞` onto `Σ𝒞` with inverse (8.65); `T` is a self-map of `V𝒞` (proof of Proposition 8.3.13). | `V𝒞` contains unbounded functions (`2u(y) + y`, for instance), on which `T` (defined on `bcℝ₊`) is not defined, so `T` is not a self-map of `V𝒞`. And `M⁻¹σ(y) = ∫₀^y u'(σ(x)) dx` can diverge: with `u(c) = 1 − 1/(1 + √c)`, which satisfies Assumption 8.3.1, and `σ(x) = x²/(2(1 + x)) ∈ Σ𝒞`, the integrand is `≍ 1/(√2 x)` near `0`. So `M` is not onto `Σ𝒞`, and Lemma 8.3.11, Propositions 8.3.12–8.3.13, Exercise 8.3.11 and the conclusion of Exercise 8.3.12 (which uses Proposition 8.3.13) are not established as printed and are not formalised. The comparison step of Exercise 8.3.12 is (`Growth.exercise_8_3_12_step`). |

## Hypotheses the book leaves implicit, and generalisations

| Where | Point | How it is stated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| §8.1.1, §8.1.3 | Offers in `W ⊆ ℝ₊`. | A measurable state space `X` with an offer `w(x)` (`JobSearch.wage`); the book's case is `X = W`, `w(x) = x`. `w ∈ L¹(φ)`; signs are needed only where stated: Exercises 8.1.7, 8.1.9 (second part) and 8.1.18–8.1.19 assume `w ≥ 0` (and `c ≥ 0`). |
| Ex 8.1.11 | `𝔼τ` for `τ = inf{t ≥ 0 : σ*(W_t) = 1}`. | Offers `W_t = w(ξ_t)` with `ξ` distributed as the product measure `φ^ℕ` (`Measure.infinitePi`); `τ` takes values in `ℕ∞` (`⊤` if no offer is accepted) and `𝔼τ` is a lower Lebesgue integral. The proof is pathwise: `τ` increases with `w*` (`firstPassage_mono`). |
| Exs 8.1.12–8.1.13 | Distributions on `ℝ₊`. | Probability measures on `ℝ` with integrable identity; Exercise 8.1.12 assumes support in `[0, M]` as printed. A mean-preserving spread is a coupling `W' = W + noise` with `𝔼[noise | W] = 0` (`IsMPS`). |
| §8.1.3.3, (8.23) | `w' = exp(z') + exp(μ + σζ')`, `z` an AR(1). | `w' = ω(z', ζ')` with `ζ'` iid from `ν` and `z` `P`-Markov with stationary `φ` (`PersistentSearch`); `ω` is measurable with `ω ∈ L¹(φ ⊗ ν)`. |
| §8.2.1 | `β(x) = b(1 − e^{−λx})`. | Any continuous, increasing `β`, concave on `ℝ₊`, with `β(0) = 0` and `β < b` (`NLDiscount.βf`); the book's function has these properties (`bexp_properties`). Offers on a general state space with `w(x) ∈ [w₁, w₂]`. |
| §8.2.2 | `γ` the risk parameter. | Any `γ ≠ 1`; the `ε` of Du's conditions is `βc/(v̄ − c)` for `γ ≥ 0` and `min(c/(1 − β), w₂)/(v̄ − c)` for `γ ≤ 0`. |
| §8.2.3 | State space `(0, M) × (0, 1)`. | `[0, M] × [0, 1]`, which `κ` maps into itself; off `(0, M)` both densities vanish and `κ = 0` (with `0/0 = 0`). `T̂` reads offers in `[0, M]`, which changes nothing where `φ_π > 0` (`LearningSearch.That_eq`). `M > 0`. |
| Prop A.5.34 | Distributions with the MLR property. | Densities `f`, `g` positive on `(0, M)`, zero elsewhere, with `g(w₂)f(w₁) ≤ g(w₁)f(w₂)` for `w₁ ≤ w₂` (no division); `u` bounded, measurable and increasing on `(0, M)` (`TwoDensities.proposition_A_5_34`). |
| Ex 8.2.10 | Distributions `F`, `G`. | Finite measures and the mixture `αF + (1 − α)G` (`exercise_8_2_10`). |
| §8.2.4 | Offers in `W`. | A general state space with `w(x) ∈ [0, M]`, `0 < α, β < 1` (`Separation`). |
| §8.3.1.2 | Functions on `[0, x̂]`; `c` continuously differentiable on `ℝ₊`. | Functions on `ℝ` vanishing off `[0, x̂]` (`NegDiscount.Val`, `NegDiscount.Policy.zero_outside`), so that the optimal policy is unique as a function; `c` differentiable on `ℝ` with continuous derivative, strictly increasing and strictly convex on `ℝ₊` (a `C¹` function on `ℝ₊` extends). |
| Thm 8.3.6, proof | Global stability of each `T_σ` and Lemma A.5.19 give order stability. | Each `T_σ` reaches its fixed point in `k₀` steps from every point, which gives order stability directly (`orderStable_of_iterate_eq`). |
| Prop 8.3.1 (iii) | `Tᵏp → p*` uniformly. | `Tᵏp = p*` for `k ≥ k₀`, hence uniformly (`ProductionChain.proposition_8_3_1`). |
| Prop 8.3.2 | Firms indexed from `1`. | `a i` is the range of firm `i + 1`, with boundaries `boundary a i = 1 − ∑_{j < i} a j` (`IsChainEquilibrium`). |
| §8.3.2 | `S`, `E` compact intervals in `ℝ₊`; revenue `ps − m(s)`, cost `c(s)`. | General measurable spaces; a bounded measurable revenue `rev(s, p)`, a bounded measurable cost, a measurable growth map `q`, and the post-harvest biomass `s₀` (the book's `0`); actions in `Bool` (`Harvest`). |
| §8.3.3, Assumption 8.3.1 | `X = A = ℝ₊`; `φ` a continuous density on `ℝ₊`; `f` strictly concave, continuously differentiable, `f(k) → ∞`. | `X = A = ℝ` with `Γ(y) = [0, y ∨ 0]` and `f((y − c) ∨ 0)` in `B`, so negative states are inert; `φ` any probability measure on `(0, ∞)`; `f` continuous, strictly increasing and concave on `ℝ₊`, differentiable on `(0, ∞)`; `u` and `f` measurable on `ℝ`. The limit `f(k) → ∞` and continuity of `f'` and of the density are not used (`Growth`). |
| Lemma 8.3.8, solution | Propositions 7.2.6–7.2.8. | Those are stated for weighted RDPs on `bℓX₊`; the same argument (Lemma A.2.6 with the closed `T`-invariant set of continuous, increasing functions concave on `ℝ₊`) is carried out in the bounded framework of Proposition 7.2.2 (`Growth.ICC`, `Growth.bellman_mem_ICC`, `Growth.lemma_8_3_8`). |
| Prop 8.3.9 | "Let `σ` be the unique `v`-greedy policy"; the proof is cited from Stachurski (2022). | Proved for any maximizing `σ` (it is unique, by strict concavity) with the differentiable sandwich of Clausen and Strub (2020): `u(t − (y − σ(y))) + β∫ v(f(y − σ(y))z) φ(dz) ≤ (Tv)(t)` near `y`, with equality at `y` (`hasDerivAt_of_concave_sandwich`). |
| Ex 8.3.9 | `𝔼_t` in (8.62). | The expectation over `ξ_{t+1} ∼ φ`: (8.63) at `y_t`, whose integrand evaluated at the realised shock is `u'(c_{t+1}) f'(y_t − c_t) ξ_{t+1}` (`Growth.exercise_8_3_9`). |
| Ex 8.3.10 | `K` maps `Σ𝒞` into itself. | `Kσ(y)` is any solution in `(0, y)` of the Euler equation with an integrable integrand (`Growth.SolvesEuler`); the order is compared at each `y` where both solutions exist. |
